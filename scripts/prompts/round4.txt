The shot: a first-person point of view from the seat of a motorcycle racing through New York City far in the future, around the year 2400, at night in heavy rain. The lower part of the frame shows the bike's cockpit: the rider's armoured gloved hands on the grips, mirrors, a glowing instrument display, rain beading on the windscreen. The city must be unmistakably New York, yet centuries beyond anything that exists today: architecture, vehicles and lighting no one has built yet. It should feel advanced and prosperous, dark and mysterious, not dystopian or grimy.

Keep New York's DNA in every frame, reimagined for the far future: the Manhattan street grid, avenue and street names (W 42 ST, 5 AV, BROADWAY) as signs or as light, rooftop wooden water towers surviving as heritage, steam rising from the street, yellow taxis reinvented, subway entrance globes, fire escapes on preserved old buildings. All signage is readable English: invented business names and generic words only (HOTEL, DELI, OPEN 24 HR, THEATRE). No real brands, logos or trademarks. Original designs, not based on any existing film, game or anime. No watermark or border.

Layered Manhattan: the old city is preserved at street level, brownstones, cast-iron facades, fire escapes and water towers, while colossal future towers kilometres tall rise straight out of and over them; stacked lanes of flying traffic stream overhead between sky bridges; small historic New York landmarks are visible far off, dwarfed by the new towers. Warm amber street level, cold blue-white above.
