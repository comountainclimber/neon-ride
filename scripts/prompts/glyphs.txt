Use your image generation tool to design an invented writing system for the signage of a far-future city in an original short film, and save it as ./signs/glyph-sheet.png (landscape, about 1536x1024). Do not write any code other than what is needed to save the file.

The script must look elegant and clean: smooth geometric strokes, circles, arcs, dots and horizontal bars, like a future calligraphy; balanced and readable as writing; never spiky, jagged, thorny or runic. No English letters or numbers, nothing resembling a real alphabet, brand or logo.

Lay out on a plain black background, glowing soft white like neon tubes:
- a grid of 24 individual glyphs;
- below it, six short sample words of 2 to 5 glyphs each, as they would appear on street signs, a road marking and a dashboard readout;
- one horizontal and one vertical sign layout using those words.

When finished, print the saved file path.
